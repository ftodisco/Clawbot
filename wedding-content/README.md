# 30-Day Wedding Content Creator Plan

A complete, ready-to-execute 30-day social media content plan for wedding content creators — covering Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest.

## What's Inside

Every day includes:
- **Topic** — the specific subject of the post
- **Hook** — the first line that stops the scroll
- **Format** — Reel, Carousel, Static, TikTok, or Stories
- **Caption** — full ready-to-post caption with personality
- **Hashtags** — 7–8 targeted hashtags per post
- **Content Tip** — production and strategy notes
- **Image Prompt** — DALL·E / Midjourney prompt for visuals

## 30-Day Structure

| Week | Theme | Days |
|------|-------|------|
| 1 | Just Engaged — The First Steps | 1–7 |
| 2 | Styling Your Vision | 8–14 |
| 3 | Vendors & Budget Mastery | 15–21 |
| 4 | Day-Of Details & After | 22–28 |
| 5 | Creator Growth & Community | 29–30 |

## Format Key

| Format | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Reel** | Short vertical video 15–60s, hook in first 3s |
| **Carousel** | Swipeable slides 5–10 frames, high save rate |
| **Static** | Single image or graphic, best for Pinterest |
| **Stories** | Ephemeral 24h — polls, Q&A, countdowns |
| **TikTok** | Trending audio, text overlay, storytelling video |

## How to Use This Plan

1. Open `content-plan.json`
2. Pick Day 1 and copy the caption, hook, and hashtags
3. Shoot or source your visual using the `image_prompt`
4. Apply the `content_tip` for production guidance
5. Schedule or post — then move to Day 2

## Niche Strategy Notes

- **Best posting times:** Tuesday–Friday, 7–9am and 6–8pm local time
- **Highest-save formats:** Carousels (checklists, tips, comparisons)
- **Highest-reach formats:** Reels and TikTok (hooks + trending audio)
- **Pinterest:** Repurpose every Carousel slide as a standalone pin
- **Engagement hack:** Week 1 and Week 3 content gets the most saves — post those on your highest-traffic days
- **Growth tip:** Day 2 (timeline), Day 5 (checklist), and Day 19 (hidden costs) are your best performing post types — repurpose them monthly
